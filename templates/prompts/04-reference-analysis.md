# Reproduce the quality of a reference

```markdown
Research how to reproduce as faithfully as possible <aspect> of the attached reference, in our existing project:
<composition, materials, reflections, depth…>, with a reproducible method compatible with <constraint: animation,
real time…>.

### 1. Analyse the reference precisely
Open the reference and examine representative enlargements (<areas>). Decompose: shapes and distribution, thickness and
details, surfaces, light and reflections, optical effects, lighting and compositing. Separate visible characteristics
from hypotheses about how they were made. Do not reproduce its inconsistencies or its text. Compare equivalent crops
with our render; show the gaps in an annotated comparison.

### 2. Research the relevant methods and experts
Yourself, without sub-agents, in several languages: breakdowns by specialized artists, official documentation,
repositories and extensions that are really useful, practitioners (specialized forums, Reddit). Check demonstrations
and technical content; a title or star count is not enough. For each resource: author, link, precise contribution,
limits, compatibility, licence, maintenance. If no skill brings a concrete benefit, say so. Reuse earlier research; do
not settle for a list of links.

### 3. Define the method suited to our project
Inspect the project before recommending. Compare credible approaches (<geometry / textures / compositing…>): fidelity,
control, stability, cost. Recommend a main approach and a fallback; explain how to obtain each characteristic. Separate
what needs geometry from what normals, materials or compositing do; check deformations on a test pattern, then on the
subject.

### 4. A usable conclusion
Illustrated diagnosis; directly applicable resources; method and settings to explore with their expected effect;
priority gaps; a minimal trial protocol (same framing, controlled variants, side by side, short animated test).
Separate documented, deduced and tested. The main criterion is a convincing visual resemblance.
```
